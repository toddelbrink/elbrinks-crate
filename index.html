    Moods
    Your moods
    Renaming a mood preserves the tag on existing records. Deleting one keeps the tag in your history (the pill just stops rendering). Reorder via drag ships in v1.1.
    Auto-tagging
    Tag untagged records (heuristic)
    Matches each untagged record against your moods' Discogs keyword lists. Free — runs locally. You review every change before anything saves.
    Re-tag all records (heuristic)
    Re-runs the heuristic across every record, including ones you've already tagged. Use after editing keywords or adding moods. Review before save — choose Merge or Overwrite.
    Suggest via Claude (untagged)
    Calls Claude Haiku for up to 200 untagged records per session (500/day limit). More nuanced than the heuristic. You review every change before anything saves.
    Compare Claude's picks (all records)
    Re-runs Claude on every record, including ones you've already tagged. Use to see Claude's suggestions alongside your current moods. Cached results don't count against the daily limit. Review before save — choose Merge or Overwrite per batch.

    About
    Crate
    —

    A vinyl listening app for Discogs collectors. Built by Todd Elbrink with Vera as co-pilot.

    Collection data from Discogs. Wikipedia bios from Wikimedia. Cover art served via Discogs CDN.

    v1.0 brings multi-user support, per-user moods + themes, mood flipping, Recent Plays, and in-app feedback. Multi-user infrastructure ships across 10 build steps; Steps 1–6 are live.
