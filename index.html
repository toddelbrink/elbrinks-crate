    About
    Elbrink's Crate
    v1.0.0-step1

    A vinyl listening app for Discogs collectors. Built by Todd Elbrink with Vera as co-pilot.

    Collection data from Discogs. Wikipedia bios from Wikimedia. Cover art served via Discogs CDN.

    v1.0 brings multi-user support, per-user moods + themes, mood flipping, Recent Plays, and in-app feedback. Multi-user infrastructure ships across 10 build steps, and this is Step 1.
